Why is remove.bg shutting down?

On December 1, 2026, remove.bg — one of the most popular background removal tools — is closing its standalone website. The service, owned by Kaleido AI, is consolidating into Canva after Canva's acquisition. Millions of users who relied on remove.bg's simple drag-and-drop interface need a free alternative.

2. Canva Background Remover

Canva has integrated remove.bg's technology and offers background removal in its design platform. The free tier allows limited removals per month; unlimited access requires a Canva Pro subscription (~$15/month). Best if you're already using Canva for design work.

  • ⚠️ Limited free uses per month
  • ⚠️ Requires account signup
  • ✅ Integrated into design workflow

3. Adobe Express Background Remover

Adobe Express offers one-click background removal. The free tier has usage limits and adds a watermark unless you subscribe to Adobe Creative Cloud. Best for existing Adobe users.

  • ⚠️ Watermarks on free tier
  • ⚠️ Requires Adobe account
  • ✅ High quality results

4. Removal.AI

Removal.AI is a dedicated background removal service. The free tier allows up to 10 image removals per month at reduced resolution. Full resolution requires a paid plan.

  • ⚠️ 10 images/month on free plan
  • ⚠️ Reduced resolution on free tier
  • ✅ Good quality AI model

5. GIMP (Free Desktop App)

GIMP is a free, open-source image editor that supports manual background removal using fuzzy select and other tools. It takes more skill than AI tools but has no limits and works offline. Best for users who want full control.

  • ⚠️ Manual — requires skill and time
  • ✅ No limits, works offline
  • ✅ Completely free, open source
